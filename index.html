About the NEURO DAILY app

NEURO DAILY is a private production tool used by a single creator to run the NEURO DAILY channel. It turns published news about artificial intelligence, science and technology into short vertical explainer videos in Russian.

How it works

  1. The tool reads public news feeds from official sources (for example company blogs, NASA, MIT News, Nature).
  2. It drafts a script based only on the source text, checks every factual statement against a verbatim quote from the source, generates a voice-over and an animated video.
  3. The creator reviews every video and approves or rejects it manually. Nothing is posted automatically without that approval.
  4. Only an approved video is uploaded to the creator's own TikTok account through the official TikTok Content Posting API, with the "AI-generated content" label enabled.

Who uses it

Only the channel owner. The app does not offer accounts, sign-up or any service to other TikTok users.

Он делает короткие объясняющие ролики о новостях ИИ и технологий по официальным источникам, проверяет факты, а публикует в собственный TikTok автора только те ролики, которые автор вручную одобрил. Другим пользователям сервис не предоставляется.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

1. Whose data

NEURO DAILY is used only by its owner. It does not collect personal data from viewers or from other TikTok users.

2. What we access on TikTok

  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic): the account's open ID and display name — only to show which account is connected.
  • Video upload and publishing (video.upload, video.publish): to upload videos the owner has approved to the owner's own account.

We do not access followers, direct messages, comments, contacts, location or payment information.

3. How it is stored

OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok are stored in the owner's private database running on the owner's own computer. They are never shared, sold or transferred to third parties, and are not written to logs. The owner's TikTok password is never seen or stored by the app — sign-in happens only on TikTok's own website.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the connection is active. Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions; the stored tokens are then deleted from the database.

5. Third parties

To produce videos the app uses: Anthropic (script drafting from public news text), Yandex SpeechKit (voice synthesis from the script text). No TikTok user data is sent to these services.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children and does not process children's data.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: send a direct message to the NEURO DAILY TikTok account.

Пароль не видим и не храним; токены лежат в частной базе владельца, никому не передаются. Отозвать доступ можно в настройках TikTok.

Effective date: November 6, 2026

NEURO DAILY is a personal content-production tool operated by the owner of the NEURO DAILY channel for their own use. No service is offered to the public and no user accounts are created.

2. Content rules

TikTok connection (for the channel owner)

After you sign in on TikTok and press Authorize, TikTok returns you to this page. The address bar of your browser then contains ?code=…&state=….

  1. Copy the whole address from your browser's address bar.
  2. Paste it into the NEURO DAILY panel → TikTok → field 2, and press Save.

The code works once and expires in a few minutes. Never share it with anyone else.

По-русски: после входа в TikTok скопируйте весь адрес из строки браузера и вставьте в панель NEURO DAILY → TikTok → поле 2 → «Сохранить».
